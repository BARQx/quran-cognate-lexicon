This tool helps you understand Qur'anic Arabic words by looking at their Hebrew cognates. Arabic and Hebrew both descend from the same Semitic root system, and Hebrew often kept older or wider senses of a root alive that classical Arabic dictionaries no longer show. Type in a root, and this tool finds its Hebrew form and looks it up in real dictionaries for you.
